# Architecture & module contracts

A first-person cab ride along a street tram line through a limestone city street.
Browser app, three.js r186 (vendored, `vendor/three`), plain ES modules with no build step.

Run: `python3 serve.py` (or any static server at the repo root) and open `http://localhost:8080/`.

## Hard rules (apply to every file)
- **Blind comparison**: never write any AI model name, AI company name or "generated by" wording anywhere: code, comments, file names, titles, metadata, commit messages, asset metadata (glTF `generator`/`copyright` fields too).
- Only `reference/*.jpg` describes the scene. Do not search the web for this street/city or for existing models of it. Never use the reference photos as textures.
- All geometry and textures are procedural (JS at runtime, or Blender/Python scripts in `tools/`), or freely licensed assets recorded in `docs/ASSETS.md` with their licence. Today everything is procedural.
- Photoreal is the goal: real-world scale, PBR values, no toon/flat/low-poly look, no primitive placeholders in the final result.
- Keep all work inside this repository. Scratch output goes to `.work/` (git-ignored).

## Tooling
- `tools/shoot.py`: headless Chrome on the GPU. It starts a private static server, opens the app, waits for `window.__ready`, saves screenshots and prints console errors and `stats` (fps uncapped, frameMs, gpuMs, draw calls, triangles, per-module chunk build ms).
  - `python3 tools/shoot.py --params "s=420&tod=0.55" --out .work/shots/x.png`
  - `--shots 4 --interval 0.5` makes a sequence. `--eval "<js>"` evaluates and prints. `--settle 3` waits longer.
- `tools/dk <cmd>`: runs a command in the Docker tools image (`docker/Dockerfile`: Blender 4.5 LTS, Node 24, Python + numpy/scipy/Pillow, ImageMagick, ffmpeg, gltf-transform), with the repo mounted at `/work`.
  - Blender: `tools/dk blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/blender/<script>.py -- <args>`. Write outputs to `assets/<area>/`.
  - `DK_GPU=1 tools/dk ...` adds the NVIDIA GPU (Cycles baking).
- URL parameters (see `src/core/params.js`): `s`, `tod`, `fov`, `speed`, `auto`, `freeze=1`, `capture=1`, `only=a,b`, `skip=a,b`, `view=s,d,h,yawDeg,pitchDeg` (free inspection camera in route coordinates; yaw + turns left), `q`, `debug=1`, `seed`.
  - Isolate your module while iterating, e.g. `only=street` (core modules materials/env/train/cameraRig always load). Add `post` to `only` to include post-processing.
  - `audio=1` forces audio in capture/headless runs, and `audiocache=0` bypasses the IndexedDB sample-bank cache.
  - Close-up inspection: `view=430,9,1.7,-60,8` stands on the right sidewalk at s=430 and looks right-ish towards the facades. Yaw −90 looks straight at the right-hand facades and +90 at the left-hand ones.

## Coordinates & conventions
- Metres, Y up, three.js right-handed. 1 UV unit = 1 metre on every library material, so geometry UVs must be in metres. Materials handle texture scale.
- **Route coordinates** `(s, d, h)`, see `src/world/route.js`. `s` is distance along the street centre line (wraps at `route.length`, about 5 km, a closed loop). `d` is the lateral offset, + to the right of travel. `h` is height above the paving. The street cross-section is level, and rail tops are flush with the paving at h = 0.
  - `route.toWorld(s, d, h, out?)`, `route.sample(s, out)` → `{pos, tan, tanH, right, heading, curvature, grade}`, `route.heading(s)`, `route.curvature(s)` (+ = left curve), `route.elevation(s)`, `route.offsetScale(s, d)` (= 1 + κd, arc-length scale of an offset line), `route.wrap`, `route.delta(a, b)`, `route.project(x, z, sHint)` → `{s, d}`, `route.segments`.
  - `object.rotation.y = route.yawAt(s)` makes local −Z point along travel (+X to the right). `rotation.y = route.heading(s)` makes local +X point along travel.
- Street cross-section (`src/core/config.js`): standard gauge 1.435 m. Rail centre lines are at `±RAIL_CENTER` (0.7455) from each track centre. Track centres are at `d = ±TRACK_OFFSET` (1.6). The camera rides the **right** track (d = +1.6). The paved track corridor spans `|d| ≤ TRACK_ZONE_HALF` (3.4), with a light stone edge band `EDGE_BAND_W` on each edge. Pole line `POLE_D` = 3.95, tree line `TREE_D` ≈ 5.15. Facades sit at `|d| = layout.halfWidth(s, side)` (≈ 11–13.5 m). Driver eye: `EYE_HEIGHT` 2.75 m. Contact wire: 6.0 m.
- **Facade frame** (per building lot): `lot.frame = {origin, uDir, nDir, up, width, ground0, ground1, floorRef, matrix}`. `u` runs along the facade and increases to the viewer's right when looking at the facade from the street. `v` is up from `floorRef`. `w` points out of the wall toward the street, so recesses are at w < 0. `frame.matrix` maps (u, v, w) to world. The frame is right-handed, so CCW winding seen from the street is front-facing. Ground (paving) height along the facade is `plan.groundAt(u)`, which is ≤ 0 because the street slopes.

## Layout (shared master plan): `src/world/layout.js`
Deterministic and computed once. **Read placements from here and never invent overlapping ones.**
- `stations[]`: `{id, s, len, stopS, platforms:[{side, s0, s1, d0, d1, h}]}`. Side platforms are 2.9 m deep and 0.30 m high, on both sides of the corridor.
- `crossovers[]`: `{s0, s1, len, from}`, a single crossover between the tracks. `from:+1` means facing points on the ride track at s0 diverging to the left track by s1.
- `crossStreets[]`: `{s, width, side (0 both | ±1), depth, angle, junction?}`. No lots, trees or poles in the mouth.
- `lots[]` (main street): `{id, side, s0, s1, len, d, seed, depth, cornerStart, cornerEnd, cornerU0, cornerU1, frame}`. `sideLots[]` line the cross streets: `{..., sideStreet:true, cross, edge, x0, x1, frame}`.
- `poles[]`: `{s, side, d, lamp, banner}`, shared by lamps and catenary. `trees[]`: `{s, side, d, seed, height, crown, mature}`.
- `cafes[]`: `{side, s0, s1, dIn, dOut, style ('umbrella'|'open'|'awning'), chairStyle, tables:[{s, d, shape, chairs:[{s, d, yaw}]}], umbrellas:[{s, d, size, color}]}`. Chair yaw is a route-relative heading offset, and the chair faces the table.
- `furniture[]`: `{type, s, d, yaw, ...}`. Types: bin, bench, bollard, planter, kiosk, advertColumn, bikeRack, infoBoard, shelter, ticketMachine, stopSign.
- `obstacles[]` (for the crowd): circles `{kind:'circle', s, d, r}` and rects `{kind:'rect', s0, s1, d0, d1}`. Query with `obstaclesNear(s, range)`.
- Per-chunk lookup: `layout.inChunk(kind, chunkIndex)` for kind in lots, sideLots, poles, trees, cafes, furniture, obstacles, crossStreets, stations, crossovers. Helpers: `halfWidth(s, side)`, `isInStation(s, m)`, `isInCrossStreet(s, side, m)`, `isInCrossover(s, m)`, `nextStation(s)`, `defaultStartS`.
- `src/world/facadePlan.js`: `facadePlan(lot)` returns the architectural plan: floors, bays, openings (kind shop/door/window/balconyDoor, top flat/round/segment/pointed, recess, shutters, lit threshold, awning, ac, flowerBox), balconies, string courses, cornice, parapet, roof, rooftop clutter, signs, flags and stone style (tint, course height, block length, rustication, quoins…). It is shared by `buildings`, `facades` and `ironwork`. Extending it is fine. Renaming fields is not. Current extensions: `plan.coursing {ch, v0}` (sills aligned per floor, heads snapped to courses); `plan.upper` (set-back penthouse, selfDressed by buildings); `plan.cuts` plus `plan.returns[]` entries (corner returns and chamfer faces with their own frames, including `isCut` and `cut`). Facades must iterate `plan.returns` generically.

## Module system: `src/main.js`
Each module is a file whose default export is an object:
```js
export default {
  name: 'street',
  async init(app) {},          // optional, once; registry order (materials, env, post first)
  build(chunk, app) {},        // optional: return an Object3D for this chunk (or null)
  dispose(obj, chunk, app) {}, // optional: default disposes non-shared geometries
  update(dt, app) {},          // optional, per frame: train → cameraRig → others in registry order
};
```
- Modules are loaded with dynamic `import()`. A module that throws at load, init, build or update is logged and disabled, and the rest keeps running. **Never statically import another module's file.** Use `app.systems.<name>` (or the aliases `app.materials`, `app.env`, `app.post`, `app.train`, `app.cameraRig`, `app.crowd`, `app.humans`, `app.audio`, `app.ui`). Static imports are fine for `src/core/*`, `src/world/{route,layout,facadePlan,streaming}.js` and `vendor/three`.
- `chunk = {index, s0, s1, lod, group, rng(tag)}`, with CHUNK_LEN = 40 m. `lod` is 0 when the chunk is closer than 200 m and 1 for the far range up to 680 m ahead. Chunks are rebuilt at lod 0 as they come close. `build` must be fast: **target < 3 ms per chunk per module** (the streamer time-slices about 7 ms per frame across modules). Precompute shared geometry in `init`. Reuse geometries (`geometry.userData.shared = true` so they are not disposed). Merge per material with `MeshBatcher` (`src/core/geom.js`) or use InstancedMesh per chunk.
- `app`: `{THREE, renderer, scene, camera, canvas, params, U, events, route, layout, world, systems, time, dt, realDt, frame, startS, cameraS, perf}`. `app.dt` is 0 when frozen, and `app.realDt` is the wall-clock delta.
- `app.world.upcoming()` lists the chunks the streamer will build next, as `[{index, lod, dist}]` nearest first, for modules that prefetch work.
- **Ground**: `route.surface(s, d)` shapes the ground outside the track corridor: sidewalk cross-fall, a hillside tilt and settlement undulation, level beyond about the facade line. `route.toWorld(s, d, h)` and `route.groundY(s, d)` include it, so always place objects with toWorld (h = height above the local paving). Never use `route.sample().pos` plus your own offsets for ground contact. The street mesh adds its own millimetre detail (gutters, crowns) on top.
- `window.__app.stats()` returns perf counters. Startup builds chunks within 240 m, then sets `window.__ready`.

## Shared runtime
- `src/core/uniforms.js` `U`: `uTime`, `uWind` (xz dir), `uWindStrength`, `uNight` (0 day to 1 night: drives lamps, shop and window emissives), `uSunDir`, `uSunColor`, `uCameraPos`, `uSpeed`. Inject them into custom shaders via `onBeforeCompile` (`shader.uniforms.uTime = U.uTime`). `env` owns the values of wind, night and sun.
- `src/core/events.js` (`app.events`): `bell {auto}`, `bell-request {urgency}`, `station-arrive {station}`, `station-depart {station}`, `doors {open}`, `tod {t}`, `ready`.
- `src/core/rng.js`: `RNG.from(...parts)`, `hash2`, `noise1`. Never use `Math.random()` for world content, because content must be identical every visit.
- `src/core/geom.js`: `ribbonGeometry`, `sweepProfile` (CCW profile in (d, h)), `MeshBatcher`, `normalizeAttributes`, `setColor`.

## Materials: `app.materials` (`src/materials/materials.js`)
- `get(name, opts?)` returns a shared material. `opts` (for example `{vertexColors: true}`) returns a cached variant. Use vertex colours for per-building stone tint and baked AO.
- `register(name, material)` adds your own material to the library. `track(material)` is **required for every material you put in the scene**, so the lighting system can configure it (shadows/CSM/fog). `onTrack(fn)`, `all()`, `names()`.
- Names (all must exist): `stone.ashlar`, `stone.smooth`, `stone.rough`, `paving.street`, `paving.sidewalk`, `paving.band`, `paving.platform`, `rail.head`, `rail.side`, `rail.groove`, `metal.iron`, `metal.pole`, `metal.galvanized`, `metal.steel`, `metal.wire`, `glass.shop`, `glass.window`, `glass.lamp`, `fabric.awning`, `fabric.umbrella`, `fabric.flag`, `wood.shutter`, `wood.door`, `wood.bench`, `paint.frame`, `plastic.chair`, `concrete`, `roof.tile`, `roof.membrane`, `plaster`, `bark`, `leaf`, `rubber`, `asphalt`, `sign.panel`, `emissive.warm`.
- Facade stone UVs: u = metres along the wall, v = metres up from `floorRef` (or world height), so ashlar courses line up across a facade.
- If you patch a material with `onBeforeCompile`, **chain** any existing hook (`const prev = m.onBeforeCompile; m.onBeforeCompile = (s, r) => { prev && prev(s, r); ... }`) and set `customProgramCacheKey`.

## System contracts
- **env** (`src/env/env.js`): sky, sun, environment map, shadows, fog/haze, wind, time of day. `env.setTimeOfDay(t)` with t in [0, 1]: 0 midday, 0.55 golden hour, 0.8 sunset, 1 dusk. `env.state = {t, sunDir, sunElevation, night}`. It sets `U.uNight`, `U.uSunDir`, `U.uSunColor` and `U.uWind*`, and emits `tod`. It owns dynamic lamp lights (a few real lights reassigned to the nearest lamps at dusk) and the headlight beam.
- **post** (`src/env/post.js`): `render(dt, app)`, `setSize(w, h)`. Owns AA, AO, DOF, bloom, tone mapping and grading.
- **train** (`src/ride/train.js`): `app.train = {s, speed (m/s), accel, throttle (−1..1), auto, state ('run'|'brake'|'dwell'|'depart'), nextStop, curvature, bellAt, t, holding, coasting}`. `s` is the cab front along the route. `t` is the simulation clock. `holding` is true until the world is ready and, in the interactive app, until the start click: the tram doesn't move meanwhile. An interactive run without `speed=` starts standing and departs with a bell after the click. `station-arrive` and `doors` carry `{visit}`, and `station-depart` fires when the tram actually moves off. A `bell-request` urgency is held for 3 s.
- **cameraRig** (`src/ride/cameraRig.js`): sets `app.camera` from the train state and **must set `app.cameraS`**. The camera is body-mounted: the eye is 1.7 m behind the cab front, so `cameraS = train.s − 1.7`, and it carries sway, bob, joint impacts and mouse look. It exposes `yaw`, `pitch`, `fov` (16:9-equivalent vertical), `prevViewProj`, `cut` and `shutterSec` for post motion blur. **The tram is never rendered: no cab, console or windscreen at any zoom.** The shared axle table lives in `src/core/config.js` (`AXLES`).
- **humans** (`src/crowd/humans.js`) renders the figures, and **crowd** (`src/crowd/crowd.js`) decides where they go:
  - `app.humans.alloc(count)` then `app.humans.set(i, {x, y, z, heading, speed, phase, pose, look, appearance, visible})` per frame for active agents, or `setVisible(i, false)`. `pose`: 0 walk/stand blend by speed, 1 stand idle, 2 sit. `appearance` is an integer seed: the renderer derives sex, height (1.50–1.95 m), build, skin, hair and clothing from it. `heading` is world yaw in radians where 0 faces −Z, matching three.js `rotation.y`. The renderer exposes `strideLength(appearance, speed)` so crowd can advance `phase` (0..1 per gait cycle) without foot sliding, and `heightOf(appearance)`.
  - **Mobility extension (M2b)**: `pose` codes 3 = pushing a stroller, 4 = riding a bicycle, 5 = walking beside a bicycle. `prop` = 0 none, 1 stroller, 2 bicycle, and `propSeed` picks the model variant and colour. Props are rendered and animated by humans, attached to the figure: bicycle wheels roll with distance, cranks turn with `phase`, and stroller wheels roll. `app.humans.cycleLength(appearance)` gives metres per crank revolution, so crowd advances phase by speed / cycleLength while cycling. `strideLength(appearance, speed, pose)` covers the walking poses. Footprints for avoidance: a bicycle is about 1.8 m long centred on the rider, and a stroller extends about 1.1 m ahead of the person. humans also builds the **parked bicycles** (its own `build(chunk)`) at `bikeRack` furniture positions and occasionally leaning on poles/trees, so one bicycle model serves both uses.
  - crowd must use `layout.obstacles` and cafés (seated patrons sit at `cafes[].tables[].chairs`), platforms (waiting passengers), and `app.train` to clear the tracks ahead of the tram. Nobody may ever intersect the camera. It emits `bell-request` and reacts to `bell`.
- **audio** (`src/ride/audio.js`): WebAudio synthesis only. It starts on the first user gesture (`app.events` `start` from ui) and reads `app.train` (speed, curvature, state) and `app.camera`.
- **ui** (`src/ride/ui.js`): DOM in `#ui-root`. Shows the speed readout, throttle state, time-of-day slider and FOV control, H to hide, and a start overlay ("click to ride"), which emits `start`. Everything is hidden when `capture=1`.

## Performance budget (1920×1080; quality first, 30 fps minimum, 60 fps nice-to-have)
- The owner accepts 30 fps. Photorealism has priority: spend headroom on image quality (shadow resolution and softness, AO, AA, geometric detail, crowd density) as long as the whole frame stays ≤ ~28 ms GPU / ≤ 20 ms CPU on this machine (RTX 4060 Ti) and riding has no hitches > 60 ms.
- Draw calls under ~1500 including shadow passes. Keep triangles reasonable (under ~10 M including shadows) and avoid aliasing at all costs.
- Per chunk per module: build under 3 ms (lod 0), under 1 ms (lod 1). Prefer instancing and merged geometry. LOD 1 (over 200 m) should be much lighter: no small details that would sub-pixel alias at that distance.
- Thin geometry (wires, railings) must not alias or shimmer. Use pixel-width-aware shading or fade for far wires, and alpha-to-coverage or textures for fine ironwork far away.

## Contract additions (M3 integration)
**URL parameters.** `sky=streaks|cumulus|haze` picks the cloud regime; the default comes from the seed (`env.weather`). `tram=<s>` puts the first oncoming tram's nose at s. `prof=1` turns on post's per-section GPU timers.

**Oncoming trams** (`src/ride/trams.js`, `src/ride/trams/**`, ride owner). `app.trams.units[]` = `{id, s, speed, accel, dir (−1), d (−TRACK_OFFSET), length (32), halfWidth (1.325), state, phase, bellAt, active}`. `s` is the leading nose on the route; the body trails towards +s, and the unit travels towards −s. They drive the same vehicle model as the cab tram, stop at the left platforms and brake for people. Event `tram-bell {unit, s, d}`. The crowd must keep the left track clear ahead of each unit, the same way it does for the cab tram. The cab tram itself is still never rendered.

**Camera.** At 20° FOV or narrower the gaze turns 70% towards the track 80 m ahead (telephoto vanishing-point framing); at 40° and wider the view stays body-mounted. Drag look is grab-style. `cameraRig.blurMaxPx` is the motion-blur streak cap in px at 1080 lines. Keys: `M` mutes, `F` toggles full screen.

**Ground contact.** Within 0.9 m of a facade the paving meets the building's own ground line (lot frame chord, `plan.groundAt(u)`). Objects standing against facades use `plan.groundAt` or `app.systems.street.heightAt(s, d)`, not toWorld with h = 0. Corner returns and cut faces have a varying `groundAt(u)` (`frame.ground0 ≠ frame.ground1`), eased into the side street.

**facadePlan extensions.** `o.backDepth` (interior box depth behind glazing), `plan.relief`, exports `facadePlan.archSegments(o, q)` and `ornament(plan, rng)`. Backdrop lots from `buildings.extraLots()` are dressed by facades (no `selfDressed`). Buildings owns door thresholds and steps. Facades dresses café 'awning' terraces with a continuous awning (hem about 2.7 m).

**Layout.** `cafes[].tables[].dressed`: 0 bare, 1 laid, 2 in use. Furniture writes it at init; the crowd seats patrons at dressed 2 first, then occasionally 1, never 0. Furniture relaxes chair and umbrella positions at init and writes them back into the layout.

**Shadows (env).**
- The sun uses 4 cascades in one atlas row. Cascades 0-1 redraw every frame, cascade 2 every 2nd frame and cascade 3 every 4th; all redraw on camera or sun moves.
- Cascades 2-3 skip `mesh.userData.thinCaster` meshes and casters under 1.5 texels. A numeric `thinCaster` is the largest cascade texel (m) at which the caster still draws.
- Static casters in a ready chunk are merged in the background into depth-only proxies. Set `mesh.userData.dynamicCaster = true` to keep a mesh out of merging; changes are detected automatically anyway.
- Instanced casters may set `mesh.userData.shadowRange = [minViewDepth, maxViewDepth, margin]` each frame so cascades they cannot reach skip them.
- The shared light loop skips dark lights (colour 0) and spot lights outside their cone or range, so permanent light slots cost nothing when off.
- `env.skyIrradianceH` is the sky irradiance luminance on a level surface; shaders may read it instead of sampling the environment map.

**Post.**
- Camera tone curve with AgX colour, plus luma sharpening; `env.exposureMax` caps metered exposure at low sun.
- A canyon-bounce hemisphere light, owned by env, adds warm fill from sunlit stone.
- Camera-only motion blur uses `cameraRig.prevViewProj`, `cut`, `shutterSec` and `blurMaxPx`.
- SSAO scales `app.materials.uniforms.aoScale` to 0.6 while it runs.
- DOF models a 1"-type sensor: f/8 by day, f/2.8 at dusk.

**Materials.**
- Extra names: `paint.frameDetail`, `wood.shutterDetail`.
- `paving.street` accepts a `laneAttr` option.
- Program keys are 'sf7' plus feature letters in lite and full families; the library field `fx` overrides the family.
- `materials.init` no longer waits for the bake; `materials.ready` resolves when the maps are final.
- Night emission scales with `app.materials.uniforms.emissiveGain`.
- 'ironwork.ac' no longer exists (AC units use 'ironwork.parts').

**Shared-batch pattern (ironwork, reusable).** `build()` returns an empty marker group, and the meshes live in one scene-level group. A chunk's content starts drawing when its marker group becomes visible (or when it replaces an upgraded chunk) and is released on dispose. Shadow stand-ins are culled per block with the light's cascade frustum. BatchedMeshes should create their colour texture up front, so the startup compile doesn't build programs for empty batches.

**Streaming.**
- `app.world.upcoming(sCam, lookAhead)` also lists chunks that will be wanted, or upgraded to lod 0, within `lookAhead` metres of travel.
- `stats().rideBuild` gives per-module ride-time build p50/p95/max; startup builds are excluded.
- Parts reveal under a per-frame upload budget. A module that pre-uploads vertex data can set `geometry.userData.revealBytes` to the bytes a reveal still sends.
- New chunks compile their programs in the background before they are shown.
